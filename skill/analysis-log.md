# Analysis Log

## Purpose
Prevent loss of reasoning between exploration and the final academic report.

For every material finding record:
- date/run identifier
- dataset/version
- analytical question
- variables
- method
- result
- interpretation
- confidence/limitations
- downstream impact

## Rules
- Record measured results, not assumptions.
- Link decisions to evidence.
- Never overwrite a previous finding without preserving the reason for revision.
- Distinguish exploratory observations from validated conclusions.
- Do not record invented or estimated results as facts.
